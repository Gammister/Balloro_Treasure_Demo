Game Rules

Start a round

Choose 1–3 balls and 5–10 lines. The green button shows the total stake. Only the first shot on a field is paid.

5x

Open cells

A closed green cell can reveal nothing, a multiplier, a diamond, a blue pocket or a red pocket. Balls may return to opened cells.

$

Cash out or risk

After a safe shot, take the displayed amount or continue free on the same field. A red pocket loses the current round.

Pocket

A hidden blue pocket opens when a ball stops on it, pulls the ball to the centre and releases three free white balls.

x10 BOOST

Collect three diamonds. Field multipliers become x10. Multipliers collected before the bonus keep their original value.

5–10

Lines and risk

More lines make cells smaller and increase available multiplier values. Volatility and possible rewards rise.

AUTO

Autoplay

Autoplay starts new paid rounds with the selected stake and ball count until you switch it off.

PLAYER INFORMATION

How wins are paid

The total stake is multiplied sequentially by every multiplier collected in the round. Example: 300 USD × 1.20x × 1.50x = 540 USD.

RTP

The theoretical RTP is 97.45%. RTP is the long-term average return calculated across a very large number of rounds; it does not guarantee the result of a specific bet, session or player. RTP formula: total payouts ÷ total stakes × 100%.

Maximum payout

The maximum multiplier in one cell is 100x after x10. The round cashout has no fixed cap because every collected multiplier is applied sequentially.

Responsible play

18+. Gambling can cause addiction. Play responsibly.
