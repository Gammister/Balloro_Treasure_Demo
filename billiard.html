ABOUT THE GAME

BalloroX is an online probability game with a fixed mathematical model. Choose a stake, 1–3 balls and 5–10 lines. Each ball has its own stake and can stop on an empty cell, a multiplier cell or enter a pocket that releases three white balls. A released ball can rarely enter a pocket again; the chance decreases at each generation.

How wins are paid

The round win is the sum of all ball payouts: ball stake × cell multiplier. x10 BOOST multiplies main-field wins. A ball that enters a pocket releases three white balls, and each one resolves on the main field with an ordinary cell multiplier or can rarely enter a pocket again. EX MULTI adds extra multiplier cells to the main field, but it does not guarantee a win.

The theoretical RTP is 97.45%. RTP is the long-term average return calculated across a very large number of rounds; it does not guarantee the result of a specific bet, session or player. RTP formula: total payouts ÷ total stakes × 100%.

The theoretical maximum win is 8100x the total stake. It requires 10 lines, x10 BOOST, the maximum allowed chain of repeated pocket entries and every final released ball landing on a 300x multiplier. This outcome is extremely rare, but non-zero.

Volatility

Fewer lines create larger targets, more frequent hits and lower maximum wins. More lines create rarer hits, higher volatility and larger possible wins.

Player information

Game rules disclose the RTP, maximum win and bonus conditions. BalloroX is intended for adult players. Play responsibly.
